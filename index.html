03

Field
notes.

I started scripting Roblox in 2020 because I wanted to know how games actually worked underneath.

Then I got stuck on the parts you can't see — the server, the datastore, the exact moment a rejoin lets someone duplicate an item. That's still the part I like most.

In 2023 I started building outside Roblox too: Discord and Telegram bots, Node.js services, external databases, webhooks, and anything the Roblox APIs will let me reach. That turned out to be the useful combination — most Roblox developers stop at the client, and most backend developers have never touched Luau. Sitting in both is why the integration work keeps coming to me.

These days I'm head developer at bwush and lead programmer on AFK for free UGC. I'd rather hand over something I'd put my name on than something delivered fast and quietly broken.
